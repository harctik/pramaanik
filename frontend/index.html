PRAMAANIK
Prototype with sample data

Follow public money from sanction to last mile.

PRAMAANIK is a prototype ledger for government schemes. Treasury staff record disbursements, auditors review flagged payments, and anyone can trace where a payment went.

Sign-in with a Privado ID wallet, or simulate it

Test sign-in. Appears only on this computer. It opens the Admin portal with sample data.

Identity

The sign-in screen builds a Privado ID (iden3comm) authorization request and shows it as a QR code. In this demo you can continue without a wallet, and no proof is checked.

Records

Schemes, disbursements, flags and anchors shown in the portals are sample records bundled with the site. They are not live government data.

Oversight

Flagged payments carry a risk score and an explanation, so an auditor can see why a payment was held before acting on it.
